# Financial Engineering Research

Code and papers from my work as a **Financial Engineering Research Assistant** at the University of Michigan (June 2025 – Present), split into two areas:

## [`/options-pricing`](./options-pricing)
A Black-Scholes PDE pricing engine (Crank-Nicolson, matching the closed-form price to 99.976% accuracy) and an implied volatility solver using Newton's method against live options chain data.

## [`/mechanism-design`](./mechanism-design)
Two papers on mechanism design and hierarchical principal-agent theory, presented to a 10-person faculty-led research group and feeding into an ongoing publication on incentive structure design for hierarchical multi-agent models.
