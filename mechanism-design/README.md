# Mechanism Design & Hierarchical Contract Theory

Two research papers exploring how principal-agent (contract) theory extends to hierarchical, multi-agent organizational structures — written as part of a UMich faculty-led research group, and presented to a 10-person team.

## Papers

- **[Mechanism Design and Its Application to Hierarchical Multi-Agent Systems](./mechanism-design.md)** — solo-authored primer on mechanism design, its history (Hurwicz, Myerson, Maskin), and its application to multi-agent systems where no single agent controls the collective outcome.
- **[Dynamic Multi-Hierarchical Organizations: Literature Review](./dynamic-hierarchical-organizations-literature-review.md)** — co-authored with Kushaal Sharma. A broader literature review spanning continuous-time dynamic incentive models (Holmström–Milgrom, Sannikov) through to recent work on dynamic incentives in multi-layer hierarchies (Miller & Yang, Hubert, Scheid et al.).

## Context

This work fed into an ongoing publication effort adding incentive structure design for hierarchical multi-agent models, presented to a 10-person faculty-led research group at the University of Michigan.
